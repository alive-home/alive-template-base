## Gotchas

- `apps/web/src/routeTree.gen.ts` is generated by the TanStack Router plugin yet committed on purpose: CI typechecks a fresh checkout before any build, and the web typecheck fails without it. Biome skips it.
- Plain `docker buildx bake` also merges `compose.yaml`, whose required `AUTH_SECRET` interpolation fails outside local dev. Every script and workflow passes `-f docker-bake.hcl` for that reason.
- The Alive web dev service runs without `.env` on purpose: its `VITE_API_BASE_URL=http://localhost:3001` would send browser calls past Vite's `/rpc` proxy to a localhost the user's browser cannot reach.
- bun's isolated linker gives every workspace package its own `node_modules`. A new workspace belongs in `[setup].keep` in `alive.toml`, otherwise every new chat reinstalls it.
